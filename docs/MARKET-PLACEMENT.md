# Market Placement

Last reviewed: 2026-06-13

SellRight is an owned commerce backend for multi-brand operators.

It is not a Shopify clone for beginners, not a WordPress plugin, and not a general enterprise commerce framework. It is a narrow backend for operators who would otherwise run several stores across Shopify, Vendure, WooCommerce, or custom stacks and absorb the duplicated cost of each one.

## Category

SellRight sits between:

- hosted commerce platforms, such as Shopify and BigCommerce;
- open-source commerce frameworks, such as Vendure, Medusa, and Saleor;
- WordPress commerce, such as WooCommerce;
- custom in-house backend builds.

The practical category:

**Owned multi-store commerce backend.**

## Ideal Customer Profile

SellRight is for operators who have:

- two to ten brands or storefronts;
- a technical owner or small technical team;
- custom payment, catalog, fulfillment, or licensing needs;
- enough order volume or platform friction that SaaS constraints matter;
- a preference for owning data, code, deployment, and gateway integrations.

## Not For

SellRight is not the right fit for:

- a first-time merchant who needs fastest time to first sale;
- a non-technical operator who wants managed support;
- a large enterprise that needs global tax, marketplaces, ERPs, and vendor support from day one;
- a team that wants GraphQL as the primary contract;
- a store whose needs are already solved cleanly by Shopify.

## Primary Wedge

The wedge is the brand-portfolio operator:

> One owner, multiple stores, repeated commerce plumbing, too much lock-in or duplicated infrastructure.

That buyer does not need a universal commerce platform. They need one backend that can operate several brands cleanly.

## Why Now

The pressure points are clear:

- hosted platforms are convenient but constrain data model, checkout, gateway choices, and cost structure;
- open-source frameworks still bring framework-specific ceremony;
- GraphQL-first APIs can become friction for simple storefront and admin flows;
- operators with several brands repeat the same backend work over and over.

SellRight converts that repeated work into one owned system.

## Positioning Statement

SellRight is the owned commerce backend for multi-brand operators who want one REST-first, Postgres-backed commerce system across their stores, with database-enforced tenant isolation, a full admin, and explicit control over the money path.

## Proof Points To Build

The market story is only credible after dogfooding proves it:

1. One real brand running live checkout.
2. Two brands running from one backend.
3. Demonstrated rollback/reconciliation path.
4. Backup and restore proof.
5. Gateway-backed refund proof.
6. Public OpenAPI docs.
7. A migration guide from Vendure or Shopify-style data.
