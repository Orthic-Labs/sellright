## What the page should carry, from the sheet alone

Several stores from one backend on your own server; one command to install, your browser to claim it; Stripe, NMI and Sezzle; the order life cycle a real store needs (edit paid orders, ship in parts, take returns, run promotions and gift cards and points); a Qwik storefront and a React admin; free in production up to 25 people; source you can read. The founder line (six years of WooCommerce, then Vendure, then this) is yours to write; the Vendure importer backs it.
